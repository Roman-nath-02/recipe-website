Title: Fluffy Pancakes

Description:
Light and fluffy pancakes perfect for a weekend breakfast.

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Servings: 4
Category: Breakfast
Tags: breakfast, pancakes, easy

Ingredients:
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 3 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/4 cups milk
- 1 egg
- 3 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions:
1. Whisk together flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
2. Mix milk, egg, and melted butter.
3. Combine wet and dry ingredients.
4. Heat a lightly greased skillet.
5. Pour batter onto skillet.
6. Cook until bubbles form.
7. Flip and cook until golden.

Notes:
Serve with maple syrup and fresh berries.
