Title: Classic Lasagna

Description:
Layers of pasta, meat sauce, and melted cheese baked until bubbly.

Prep Time: 30 minutes
Cook Time: 1 hour
Servings: 8
Category: Dinner
Tags: pasta, italian, comfort food

Ingredients:
- 12 lasagna noodles
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 28 ounces crushed tomatoes
- 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning
- 15 ounces ricotta cheese
- 2 cups mozzarella cheese, shredded
- 1/2 cup parmesan cheese
- Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. Cook noodles according to package directions.
2. Brown beef with onion and garlic.
3. Add tomatoes and seasoning; simmer 20 minutes.
4. Spread sauce in baking dish.
5. Layer noodles, ricotta, sauce, and cheeses.
6. Repeat layers.
7. Top with remaining mozzarella.
8. Bake at 375°F for 45 minutes.
9. Rest 15 minutes before serving.

Notes:
Tastes even better the next day.
